Objective : To analyze an EV (Electric Vehicle) Population Dataset to offer insights on the current landscape and ground reality of BEVs (Battery Electric Vehicles) and PHEVs (Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles) in order to assess their markets' size and growth.

Methodology :-
1. Meticulously cleaned and processed the data for accuracy and reliability.
2. Engineered key metrics such as Total vehicles by model year (from 2010 onwards), Total vehicles by State, Top 10 Total Vehicles by Make, Total Vehicles by CAFV Eligibility and Top 10 Total Vehicles by Model to be included in the dashboard.
3. Developed new measures (Total Vehicles, Avg Range, BEVs, % Of BEVs, PHEVS and their % among the Total Vehicles) using DAX in order to show them as KPIs for streamlined data comprehension.
4. Used different types of customized visualizations to visualize the intricate relationships among the various parameters/columns and enabled user driven parameters for visualization.
